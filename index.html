WHO WE ARE

About Our Project

PaperLess Campus is an educational awareness project focused on reducing paper waste in college. Our aim is to understand how paper is used, identify common causes of paper wastage and encourage students to adopt more responsible habits.

Paper is an important part of education, but unnecessary printing, unused notebook pages, discarded assignments and one-sided printing can lead to avoidable waste. By spreading awareness and encouraging practical alternatives, we hope to inspire students and teachers to make better choices.

Through this project, we propose simple changes such as using digital notices, submitting assignments online whenever permitted, reusing unused pages and recycling waste paper. These steps can help our college move towards a more environmentally responsible future.

UNDERSTANDING THE ISSUE

Why Should We Reduce Paper Waste?

Paper waste may seem like a small issue, but unnecessary consumption can contribute to resource use and increase the amount of waste produced on campus.

📄

Unnecessary Printing

Printing documents that could be shared digitally can use paper without providing an extra benefit.

📚

Unused Notebook Pages

Partially used notebooks and blank pages may be discarded even when they could still be useful.

🗑️

Paper in General Waste

When paper is mixed with other waste, it can become harder to collect and recycle properly.

SMALL ACTIONS, BIG DIFFERENCE

Our Proposed Solutions

PaperLess Campus promotes practical and realistic methods that students, teachers and college departments can consider adopting to reduce unnecessary paper use.

📱

Digital Notices

Share announcements, timetables and event information through college groups, email or other approved digital platforms instead of printing multiple copies.

💻

Online Assignments

Encourage online assignment submission wherever teachers and college rules permit. Digital submissions can reduce unnecessary printing.

📝

Use Both Sides

Print on both sides of a sheet when appropriate. This can reduce the number of sheets needed for suitable documents.

♻️

Reuse and Recycle

Reuse blank notebook pages for rough work and collect recyclable paper separately instead of throwing it away with mixed waste.

📢

Spread Awareness

Organise awareness activities, posters and discussions to help students understand the importance of responsible paper consumption.

🌱

Responsible Habits

Before printing, check whether a digital copy is sufficient. Print only what is needed and avoid wasting unused sheets.
